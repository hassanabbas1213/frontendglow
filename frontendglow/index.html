FAQs

Questions? Look here.

What type of AI-powered websites do you build?

I build fully customized, scalable websites integrated with AI capabilities. This includes AI-driven search, dynamic content personalization, and intelligent lead generation forms designed to convert visitors into active customers.

Can you integrate an AI chatbot into my website?

Yes, absolutely! I can integrate intelligent chatbots trained on your business data to handle customer inquiries 24/7, book appointments, and seamlessly capture leads without human intervention.

How does an AI calling agent work?

An AI calling agent uses natural language processing to conduct human-like phone conversations. It can make outbound calls to qualify leads or handle inbound customer support calls, freeing up your team's time.

Can you automate repetitive business tasks with AI?

Yes. By leveraging tools like Make, Zapier, and custom APIs, I can connect your software stack and automate data entry, email follow-ups, and CRM updates to save you hundreds of hours.

How long does an AI website or automation project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity. A standard AI-integrated website takes 1-3 weeks, while complex automation setups might take 2-6 weeks. We will establish a clear timeline during our initial strategy phase.
